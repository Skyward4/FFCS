Faculty selection flowcharts

Each path runs left to right: OOPS → DSA → CS → OS → Physics. No two faculty on a path share a lab slot.

Theory slots (A1, B1, C1, E1, F1, G1 …) are all different letters across subjects, so only the lab (L) slots can clash. Maths has no lab slots, so any of its three faculty fits every branch; they all share slot A1, so pick one.
